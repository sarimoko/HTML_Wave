METHOD NOTES & DEFINITIONS 07
dBFS
Decibels relative to digital full scale. 0 dBFS represents maximum representable digital amplitude.
RMS
Root mean square amplitude, calculated from the sampled buffer.
Crest factor
Peak amplitude divided by RMS amplitude.
Zero-crossing estimate
A rough estimate from positive-going zero crossings. Most useful for clean single tones; not a substitute for spectral analysis.
Calibration
Amplitude is normalized digital sample level, not calibrated volts or sound-pressure level. Microphone sensitivity and system gain are unknown.
